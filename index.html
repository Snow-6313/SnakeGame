❓ HOW TO PLAY

🎮 Controls

  • Arrow Keys or WASD — Move the snake up, down, left, right
  • You cannot reverse direction — turning 180° instantly kills you!
  • Eat the orange food to grow longer and earn points
  • Avoid hitting walls or your own body — both end the game
  • Reach the score target shown at the top to win the level and progress

📊 Scoring & Multipliers

All multipliers stack together — your final points = base × streak × length × event multipliers.

  • 🟠 Orange food = 10 pts base
  • ✨ Bonus food (white/glowing) = 25 pts base — spawned by events
  • 🔥 Streak Multiplier — eating 3+ foods in a row without stopping builds a streak. Every 3 eats adds +0.5× (e.g. 6 eats = 2×, 9 eats = 2.5×). Shown at the top when active.
  • � Length Multiplier — the longer your snake, the bigger the bonus. Watch the green bar under the canvas. Longer snake = more multiplier!
  • ⚡ Event Multipliers — some events (Double Points, Triple Points, etc.) add an extra stack on top of everything else

Example: eating food with a 2× streak, 1.5× length, and a 2× event active = 10 × 2 × 1.5 × 2 = 60 pts!

🏆 Win Levels

  • Each level has a score target (starts at 1000 pts). Hit it to win!
  • After winning, you continue with a harder target and bigger multiplier tiers
  • Each win level makes the length multiplier scale faster and higher

💡 Tips

  • Build a streak by eating food consecutively — every 3 eats adds +0.5× to all points
  • Keep your snake long for a higher length multiplier — watch the 🐍 green bar fill up below the canvas
  • During ⚠️ harmful event warnings, move to open space so you have room to react when it hits
  • Use Ghost Mode 👻 aggressively — cut through your own body to reach food you normally couldn't
  • Don't panic during Reverse/Swap Controls — slow down mentally and think one move at a time
  • During Bomb Food 💥, real bombs look slightly different from normal food — learn to spot them!
  • The Mystery Box 🎁 is almost always worth eating — the rewards usually outweigh the risk
  • All multipliers stack — a streak + long snake + point event can make each food worth hundreds of points!
